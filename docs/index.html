Hedgehogs — Privacy Policy

The U7 training app for coaches, families, and players.

Effective date: September 4, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The Hedgehogs app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no registration, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

The app simply displays the training plan content on your device.

How the app works

Everything runs locally on your device. The session timer, the training plan, and the diagrams are all stored inside the app itself — nothing about how you use the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

Third-party links

Some activities include links to demo videos that open in YouTube. Opening a video is an opt-in action, and once you leave the app, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to the YouTube website or app. The Hedgehogs app does not send any information to YouTube or Google.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for the coaches and families of U7 soccer players. It is not directed at children and does not offer accounts to anyone, including children. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Data storage & security

Since the app does not collect or store any personal data, there is nothing to store, transmit, or secure — and nothing that can be lost or misused.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and the effective date above will be updated.

Contact
